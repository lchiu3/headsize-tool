Headphone Sizing Tool

We will measure your Head Breadth and Head Height using your webcam to categorize your head size.

Calibration

We need your average eye distance (IPD) to scale the image.

Part 1: Head Breadth

1. Place your Index Fingers on both Tragus (ear bumps).
2. Use the Bubble Level to balance your head.
3. Look straight at the camera.

Part 2: Head Height

1. Place one finger on your Tragus.
2. Press your Flat Palm firmly on the Top of your Head.
3. Center the Bubble Level.
